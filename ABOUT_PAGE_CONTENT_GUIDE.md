## Writing Style Guidelines

### Do Use:
- **Luxury vocabulary:** curated, bespoke, frictionless, strategic, heritage, vision, orchestrate, seamless
- **Short, punchy sentences:** "This is real estate as it should be."
- **Specific details:** "the third-wave coffee roasters redefining Marietta Square"
- **Active voice:** "I provide" not "clients are provided with"

### Don't Use:
- **Real estate clichés:** "passionate about helping people," "dream home," "going the extra mile"
- **Generic statements:** "I work hard for my clients"
- **Passive voice:** "Homes are sold by me"
- **Exclamation points:** Keep tone sophisticated, not salesy
